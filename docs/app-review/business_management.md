# business_management

## Use case
Optional. The Connected Platforms screen adds this scope only when the operator checks the business-manager box. It lets Helixa list businesses and the Pages inside them when the Pages are not visible from `pages_show_list` alone. Helixa does not create ad accounts, assign partners, or edit business settings.

## Screencast
1. Open Connected Platforms and enable the business-manager checkbox before connecting Facebook.
2. Show the consent screen including `business_management`.
3. Show the Pages that belong to that business and connect one of them.
4. Leave the checkbox off in a second take and show that the basic Page list still works without this scope.
5. Do not open Ads Manager or change a business setting.
