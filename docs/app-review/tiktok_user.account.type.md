# user.account.type

## Use case
Check that the authorized account is a Business Account before Cyreax DM offers Business Messaging. A personal account is not given a messaging automation.

## Screencast
1. Authorize a Business Account and show Cyreax DM accepting the connection.
2. If a personal account is available, show Cyreax DM refusing messaging for it, or read the account type stored on the connection.
3. Do not convert the account type inside the recording.
