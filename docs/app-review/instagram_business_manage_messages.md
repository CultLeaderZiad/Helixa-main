# instagram_business_manage_messages

## Use case
Receive Instagram direct messages and send the replies the creator configured: keyword automations, flow steps, AI answers from their knowledge base, and human replies from the inbox. Messages are stored in the creator's workspace so the team can continue the thread. Helixa sends a message only after the person messages the business, or as the private reply that Instagram allows from a comment the person wrote.

## Screencast
1. From a personal Instagram account that is not the business, send a DM containing a keyword the automation uses, for example "price".
2. In Helixa, open Inbox. The thread appears with the incoming text.
3. Show the automation or flow that matches the keyword, then show the private reply arriving in the Instagram thread.
4. Reply once from the Helixa inbox and show that single message in Instagram.
5. Do not message an account that has not started the thread.
